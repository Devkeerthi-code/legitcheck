What to look for

Scam offers repeat the same few moves.

Most fake internships in India follow a script: an exciting stipend, then a fee, a chat app and a deadline. Genuine offers look boring by comparison, and that is a good sign.

Signs of a scam

Warning signs
  • You pay before you start: a registration, training or "security deposit" fee"pay ₹1,999 to confirm your seat"
  • HR only talks on Telegram or WhatsApp, with no company number or office"contact HR on Telegram: @hr_desk"
  • A Gmail or Yahoo address claiming to be a companycompany.hr.team@gmail.com
  • You get paid to like videos, rate hotels or "complete tasks""earn ₹50 per like"
  • Aadhaar, PAN or bank details asked for before any interview
  • Shortened or lookalike links that hide where they gobit.ly/..., nex0ra-careers.co
  • Pressure to decide now"offer valid only today", "reply in 30 minutes"

Signs of a genuine offer

Consistent
  • You never pay. The company pays you.
  • There was a real interview or test with named people
  • Email comes from the company's own domain, the same one as its websitecareers@company.com
  • The role is listed on the company's official careers page
  • ID and bank details are asked for only after a signed offer, through an HR portal
  • Links go to the company's own website, in full
  • You get time to read the offer and ask questions

A clean-looking message can still be a scam, and an odd-looking one can still be real. That is why LegitCheck never says "safe". It shows you what it found and leaves the call to you.

Questions

Straight answers.

If you have already paid someone, go to the third tab now.

What does the score mean?

It is the model's estimate, from 0 to 1, of how much the message resembles postings that were labelled as fake. It is a likelihood, not proof. The flags underneath tell you why.

Can it be wrong?

Yes, in both directions. A careful scammer can write a clean message, and a small genuine company can use a Gmail address. Treat the report as a second pair of eyes, then verify.

Why does it never say "safe"?

Reading one message can't prove a company is real. The best verdict is "Looks consistent with genuine postings", which means no warning signs were found, not that there are none.

What was it trained on?

A public dataset of job postings labelled real or fake, plus hand-written rules for scam patterns common in India, like registration fees and task scams. The rules explain, the model adds a second opinion.

Is what I paste stored?

No. The text is checked and thrown away. We only count that a check happened and which verdict it got, with no text, email or link attached.

Does the extension read my pages?

Only the text you select and choose to check. It does not read pages in the background or track where you browse.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page, paste, check.

I already paid a fee. What do I do?

Call 1930 immediately. It is the National Cyber Crime Helpline. The sooner you report, the better the chance the money can be held before it moves.

Then file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.

Should I contact my bank?

Yes. Call the number on the back of your card or in your bank's official app. Tell them it was a fraud payment and ask them to raise a dispute. Keep the UTR or transaction ID.

I shared my Aadhaar, PAN or bank details.

Report it the same way, and tell your bank. You can lock your Aadhaar biometrics on the official UIDAI website or app. Watch for unexpected loan or OTP messages.

What should I save as evidence?

Screenshots of the chat, the sender's number or handle, email addresses, UPI IDs, links and payment receipts. Don't delete the conversation.

Already paid someone? Call 1930 now.

The National Cyber Crime Helpline. Reporting within the first hours gives the best chance of stopping the money. Then file at cybercrime.gov.in and call your bank.
